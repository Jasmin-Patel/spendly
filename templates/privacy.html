Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and a hashed version of your password. When you use the app, we store the expense records you create, including amounts, categories, dates, and any descriptions you provide.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide the Spendly service — authenticating your account, displaying your expense history, and generating summaries. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the app.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. Passwords are never stored in plain text — they are hashed before storage. We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal or financial data with third parties. We may use third-party infrastructure providers (such as hosting services) to operate the app, but these providers do not have access to your expense data beyond what is necessary to run the service.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. You may also export your expense data from within the app. If you have concerns about how your data is handled, you can contact us at any time.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the app. We take privacy seriously and will respond promptly.
